# Bighorn Sheep Dominance

Data record wins and losses for 28 female bighorn sheep observed on the
National Bison Range in 1984. the weight of a tie from a to b is the
number of occasions on which a was observed dominating b. Ages are
listed, but those assigned an age of 9 are at least 9 years old; they
may be older.

## Usage

``` r
sheep
```

## Format

igraph object

## Source

http://moreno.ss.uci.edu/data.html#sheep
