# Bison Dominance (weighted)

Species: *Bison bison*

Taxonomic class: Mammalia

Population type: semi-ranging

Geographical location: Moiese, Montana, USA

Data collection technique: survey scan

Interaction type: dominance

Definition of interaction: Only those aggressive interactions were
analyzed that led to an outcome in which the lost and the winner could
be categorized unambiguosly

Edge weight type: frequency

Total duration of data collection: 2 months

Time resolution of data collection (within a day): 1sec

Time span of data collection (within a day): focal follow/ad libitum
