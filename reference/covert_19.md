# Global Suicide Attacks

Data is on militant organizations between 1985 and 2006. Each node
signifies a militant organization or other type of entity that conducts
suicide attacks. 1-mode matrix for each year, organization by
organization. Undirected, binary ties represent a known physical
relationship between agents from different but “connected”
organizations.

    1985 10 x 10
    1986 4 x 4
    1990 4 x 4
    1993 4 x 4
    1994 4 x 4
