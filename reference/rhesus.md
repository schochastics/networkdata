# Rhesus Monkey Grooming

observed grooming episodes in a community of free ranging rhesus monkeys
in Cayo Santiago observed in June and July of 1963. Seven are males
(066, ER, R006, EZ, EC, CY and CN) and the other nine are females.

## Usage

``` r
rhesus
```

## Format

igraph object

## Source

http://moreno.ss.uci.edu/data#rhesus

## References

D. S. Sade, "Sociometrics of Macaca mulatta: Linkages and cliques in
grooming matrices," *Folia Primatologica*, 1972, 18: 196-223.
