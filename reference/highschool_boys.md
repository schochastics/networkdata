# Friendships among High School Boys

In the fall of 1957. and the spring of 1958. boys in a small high school
in Illinois were asked. "What fellows here in school do you go around
with most often?" The data are from research reported by Coleman. The
data report the direct choices of each of 73 boys at two times. HS1 was
recorded in 1957 and HS2 in 1958.

## Usage

``` r
highschool_boys
```

## Format

igraph object

## Source

http://moreno.ss.uci.edu/data.html#high

## Details

the edge attribute time contains the time period.

## References

Coleman, J. S. Introduction to Mathermatical Sociology. New York: Free
Press, pp.450-451.
