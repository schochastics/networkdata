# High-tech Managers (Friendships)

These data were collected from the managers of a high-tec company. The
company manufactured high-tech equipment on the west coast of the United
States and had just over 100 employees with 21 managers. Each manager
was asked "To whom do you go to for advice?" and "Who is your friend?"
Data for the item "To whom do you report?" was taken from company
documents. In addition attribute information was collected. This
consisted of the managers age (in years), length of service or tenure
(in years), level in the corporate hierarchy (coded 1,2 and 3; 1=CEO, 2
= Vice President, 3 = manager) and department (coded 1,2,3,4 with the
CEO in department 0 ie not in a department).

## Usage

``` r
ht_friends
```

## Format

igraph object
