# Kangeroos Proximity (weighted)

Species: *Macropus giganteus*

Taxonomic class: Mammalia

Population type: free-ranging

Geographical location: New South Wales, Australia

Data collection technique: survey scan

Interaction type: spatial proximity

Definition of interaction: Two individuals were assumed to be
associating if they occured within 120 cm of another at set 15-min
intervals in the enclosure.

Edge weight type: frequency

Total duration of data collection: Not specified

Time resolution of data collection (within a day):

Time span of data collection (within a day): focal follow/ad libitum
