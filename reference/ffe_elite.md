# French Financial Elite (elite)

In 1990 Kadushin collected data from 127 members of the French financial
elite. He used various criteria to determine the top 28 and recorded
their who-to-whom responses to questions about who was influencential,
who were members of the elite and who were friends. He also recorded a
large amount of information on their individual backgrounds and
characteristics.

## Usage

``` r
ffe_elite
```

## Format

igraph object

## Source

http://moreno.ss.uci.edu/data#ffe

## References

Kadushin, C. 1995. "Friendship among the French financial elite."
*American Sociological Review* 60:202-221.
