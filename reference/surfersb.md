# Windsurfers (Interactions)

This was a study of windsurfers on a beach in southern California during
the fall of 1986. The windsurfing community was fairly clearly divided
into at least two sub-communities. Members of each community seemed, to
some degree, to limit their interaction to fellow group members.
Contacts between members of the two groups occurred, but these were less
frequent. Observations of 43 individuals were made for 31 days. All
interpersonal contacts among collections of these individuals were
recorded.

## Usage

``` r
surfersb
```

## Format

igraph object
