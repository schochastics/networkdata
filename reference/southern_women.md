# Davis - Southern Women

These data were collected by Davis et al. in the 1930s. They represent
observed attendance at 14 small social events by 18 Southern women.

## Usage

``` r
southern_women
```

## Format

(bipartite) igraph object

## Source

http://moreno.ss.uci.edu/data.html#davis

## References

Breiger R. (1974). The duality of persons and groups. *Social Forces*,
53, 181-190.

Davis, A. et al. (1941). Deep South. Chicago: University of Chicago
Press.
