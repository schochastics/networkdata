# Asianelephants Dominance (unweighted)

Species: *Elephas maximus*

Taxonomic class: Mammalia

Population type: free-ranging

Geographical location: Uda Walawe National Park, Sri Lanka

Data collection technique: focal sampling

Interaction type: dominance

Definition of interaction: Indicators of dominance as well as
subordination was included. If a series of interactions occurred during
a particular event, the winners/losers were determined only on
conclusion of the event, when individuals or groups moved apart.

Edge weight type: unweighted

Total duration of data collection: 206days

Time resolution of data collection (within a day): 1sec

Time span of data collection (within a day): 5.5 hours
